The three main ways TikTok pays creators

Creator Rewards Program

10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the previous 30 days. Only original videos longer than one minute earn.

TikTok Shop Affiliate

Around 1,000 followers in most markets, plus identity verification and an account in good standing.

LIVE gifts

Around 1,000 followers to go LIVE. You must be 18 or older to receive and withdraw gifts.

Thresholds differ by market and TikTok can change them without notice. Our guide to TikTok monetization requirements by program keeps the current figures with sources.

Qualifying is not the same as getting paid

Meeting the numbers only makes a program appear in TikTok Studio. Before the first payout you still enrol and accept the terms, pass TikTok's review, and complete identity and tax verification. Creator Rewards earnings are finalised monthly and paid out the following month. If you are working towards Creator Rewards, read how many followers you need to get paid on TikTok.
